NeeDoh Fuzz Ball Guide

An independent guide to the Schylling NeeDoh Fuzz Ball, a velvet-covered squeeze toy. It comes in three sizes: the Teenie 1.5-inch 3-pack for $5.99, the Regular 2.5-inch single for $4.99, and the Super 4.5-inch single for $12.99.

Four styles are sold: Flower Power, Wonder Waves, Wild Cats and Classic. All four share the same velvet texture and slow, dough-like squish. Buyers rate the ball 4.5 out of 5 from 2,400 Amazon ratings, and it is marked for ages 3 and up.

To keep a Fuzz Ball clean, wash it with mild soap and cool water and pat it dry. Sticky tape dabs lint off, and a little cornstarch helps with sticky spots. Do not stretch it, pull it, or leave it in a hot car.
